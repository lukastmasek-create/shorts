# HROU — 54 s kinetic-type hype promo (ucimesehrou.org)

Fake brand mark for this film: **HROU.** (invented wordmark: red capsule + white spark shape + Archivo Black
letters). The real site is only ever named once, small, on the end card (`ucimesehrou.org`). No third-party
brand, logo, face, handle or watermark appears anywhere in the film.

## Facts used (from the site copy kept in `ucimesehrou/INSTAGRAM.md` and `ucimesehrou/README.md`)

- subjects for primary school grades 1–9, high school, entrance-exam prep (přijímačky), finals prep (maturita)
- every course has five levels
- every answer comes with an explanation of *why*
- daily quiz, review, escape rooms
- parents see progress; teachers assign work and see class results
- registration is free (a paid Pro version also exists, so the film says "free to start", never "everything free")
