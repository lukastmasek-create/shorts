# The Brutal Truth About YouTube
## Facts (real, approximate)
- YouTube says over 500 hours of video are uploaded every minute.
- Partner Program entry (ads): 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months.
- Creators receive 55% of long-form ad revenue; YouTube keeps 45%.
- Shorts RPM is reported in cents per 1,000 views.
- Algorithm rewards click-through and watch time, not "quality".

## Voice-over
Here is the brutal truth about YouTube. Five hundred hours of video are uploaded every single minute. Yours is one of them.

You upload your first video. You tell your mom. You refresh the page. Twelve views. Eleven are you.

Week two, you change the thumbnail. Week four, the title. Week eight, your haircut. Still twelve.

So you learn the algorithm. It does not care if your video is good. It cares if people click. So you add a shocked face, and a red arrow.

And one day, a video takes off. Then the comments arrive.

Luckily, a million people watched. Unluckily, now you must do it again every week, forever.

Here is the math. To earn anything, you need one thousand subscribers, and four thousand hours of watch time. Then YouTube keeps forty five percent of the ads. Shorts pay a few cents for a thousand views.

So you make more videos. Shorter. Faster. Louder. You sleep less. The channel grows. The joy does not.

And the funniest part? You are watching a video about quitting, and thinking: I should start a channel.

Go ahead. Worst case, you get twelve views. Eleven of them yours.
