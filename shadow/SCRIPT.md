# What If Your Shadow Had Its Own Consciousness?

## Facts (physics, all real)
- Shadow = region where your body blocks light. Nothing physical, no mass.
- Length = height / tan(sun elevation). Noon: shortest (tiny, under feet). Sun at ~7 deg: about 8x your height.
- Several light sources = several shadows (one lamp each, overlapping soft edges).
- Cloud cover or darkness removes it; it returns with light.
- Shadow always copies the shape you make; its motion is fully set by you and the light.

## Voice-over (~219 words, ~59 s at 3.7 w/s). English, one steady tone.

HOOK
Your shadow is alive. And it has been copying you your entire life.

SETTING
It is born every morning at sunrise. Long, stretched, and confused.
By noon it is tiny. Crushed under your feet, because the sun is right overhead.

ACTION
It tries to run away. But it is attached at the heels.
Every step you take, it takes. Every wave, it waves.
It has never once made a move of its own.
Then you walk past a street lamp. Suddenly it splits in two. Then three.
Now it is a crowd, and it still cannot say a single word.

SNAP
Then a cloud covers the sun. And it is gone.

DEADPAN
Luckily, it comes back when the sun does.
Unluckily, it has no memory of any of this.

EXPLANATION
Here is the thing. A shadow is not a thing at all.
It is just the place where you stop the light.
A hole in the sunshine, shaped like you.
So if it were conscious, it would be conscious of one thing only: you.

CONSEQUENCE
Every night, when the light goes out, it dies.
Every morning, you drag it back to life without asking.

CALLBACK TWIST
So tomorrow at sunset, when it stretches eight times taller than you,
walk away from the sun. Let it lead for once.
Because it has followed you your whole life.

## Shot ideas (for story.ts)
1 hook CU: kid on sunny street, shadow turns head toward camera. 2 sunrise long shadow. 3 noon top-down tiny shadow. 4 feet-attached shadow mimics walk. 5 street lamp, shadow splits x3. 6 cloud, shadow vanishes (white flash). 7 explainer: hand blocks sun, hole in light. 8 night, shadow fades. 9 golden sunset, kid walks away from sun, giant shadow leads.
