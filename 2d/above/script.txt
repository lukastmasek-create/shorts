What if you could watch your own life from above?
Brains can already fake it. In 2002, doctors stimulated the right angular gyrus of an epilepsy patient. She reported seeing her body from above.
In 2007, volunteers wore video goggles showing their own back from two meters behind. They felt as if they were sitting behind their own body.
And the view helps. When people looked at painful memories like a fly on the wall, they showed less emotional reaction.
In 2014, people reasoned more wisely about other people's conflicts than their own. Stepping back, even by using their own name, closed the gap.
So next hard choice, step outside yourself. Use your own name.
