## YouTube Shorts
Title: What If Humans Lived for 1,000 Years? #Shorts

Description:
The longest verified human life is 122 years. A thousand years is about eight of those. Human cells in a dish stop dividing after around fifty divisions, researchers disagree about a ceiling near 125 or up to 150, and a Greenland shark was dated at roughly 400 years. A thousand-year human life is a question, not a forecast.

Sources: Gerontology Research Group (Jeanne Calment); Our World in Data (life expectancy); Hayflick & Moorhead, Exp Cell Res (1961); Dong, Milholland & Vijg, Nature (2016); Pyrkov et al., Nature Communications (2021); Nielsen et al., Science (2016); Martínez, Exp Gerontol (1998).
