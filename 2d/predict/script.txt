What if an AI could predict your death? It gives you a date, and will not explain why.
Medicine already tries a version of this. In twenty eighteen, Stanford researchers trained an AI on hospital records.
It estimates who may die within three to twelve months, so palliative care teams can reach them in time.
In twenty twenty, researchers fed an AI a simple heart tracing, an ECG.
It predicted who would die within a year, even when doctors read the ECG as normal. Cardiologists could not spot the pattern it used.
In twenty twenty-three, Danish researchers went further. They gave an AI the health and work records of about six million people.
It predicted early death over four years better than other models.
But it only knew Denmark. And accidents stay unforeseeable.
So the date is not a fact. It is a probability, with error.
Would you open it, or would you rather not know?
