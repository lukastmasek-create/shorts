**YouTube Shorts title:** What If AI Could Predict Your Death?
**Description:** A 2018 Stanford model flags hospital patients likely to die in 3–12 months so palliative care can start in time. A 2020 study predicted one-year mortality from ECGs, even ones doctors read as normal. A 2023 Danish model trained on ~6 million people's records predicted early death better than other models, but only knows Denmark and can't foresee accidents. The "date" is a probability, not a fact. #shorts #ai #whatif

**Sources:** Avati et al., BMC Med Inform Decis Mak 2018 · Raghunath et al., Nature Medicine 2020 · Savcisens et al., Nature Computational Science 2023/24. Bars and grids on screen are schematic.
