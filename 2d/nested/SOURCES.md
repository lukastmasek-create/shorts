Checked by web search 2026-11-16; several pages secondary. Hook claim "one starts creating its own" is a thought experiment, not a finding: script must say "imagine". Counts of simulated worlds, layers and years on screen = illustration.

## Do NOT claim
- That we live in a simulation (Bostrom says no strong grounds to pick).
- That any AI simulates a universe, or inhabitants who are conscious; Genie 3 makes minutes of video-game-like worlds only.
- That cosmic-ray test proves/disproves simulation; it tests one lattice model.
- Any exact count of "billions of realities"; that line from the idea list is illustration only.
- That Life-in-Life layers are conscious or that real physics is a cellular automaton (Fredkin/Wolfram digital physics = unverified hypothesis, left out).
