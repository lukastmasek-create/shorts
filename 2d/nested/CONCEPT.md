# NestedShort — concept (#100 "AI That Simulates Universes")
Three framings pitched (all MONO: white line art, no faces, ~65 s):

A. **Russian-doll screens.** A rounded square "screen" holds a person; camera zooms in (zoomIn) to find a smaller screen with a smaller person, again and again. Facts hang on each layer (Genie 3, Bostrom, Life-in-Life, Lloyd budget, cosmic-ray test). Ends zoomed out: our screen sits inside another one.
B. **Grid that becomes the grid.** Pure Game-of-Life cell field; each scene zooms into a cell that is itself a field. Strong for Life-in-Life, weak for Bostrom/Genie/Lloyd.
C. **Ledger of possibilities.** A person at a desk weighing Bostrom's three options as three doors. Clear, but no "nested" image; the hook (one world starts its own) is told, not shown.

**Pick A**, with B's cell grid inside layer scenes. Why: the zoom IS the idea (nesting), matches `SceneFx zoomIn`, needs only boxes/people/grids the kit already has, every fact lands on one layer. Dropped from the idea list: any claim that inhabitants are conscious or that billions of worlds exist (no source; see SOURCES.md "do not claim").
