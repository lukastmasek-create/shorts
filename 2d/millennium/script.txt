What if you could see a thousand years into the future?
You blink, and the year is three thousand twenty-six.
The skyline is gone. Cities have grown into the sky, and deep under the ground.
The streets are silent. No cars. No crowds. Only light, moving through glass.
You look for people. But the people you know are not here.
Some live inside machines, with no body at all.
Others rebuilt their own bodies, until they look like no one you remember.
They do not speak. Thoughts pass between them like signals.
They look at you the way you look at an old photograph.
Humanity did not end. It became something else.
And your whole world, every war and every dream, is the first page of their story.
So tell me. Is this the future you are building?
