Title: What If You Could See People's Insecurities? #Shorts

Description:
What if insecurities floated above everyone's heads? You'd see them on the winners too. Research on the impostor phenomenon, the spotlight effect, the illusion of transparency, the liking gap and the "beautiful mess" effect all point the same way: others notice far less than you think.

Sources: Clance & Imes (1978); Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky, JPSP (2000); Savitsky & Gilovich, JESP (2003); Boothby et al., Psychological Science (2018); Bruk, Scholl & Bless, JPSP (2018).
