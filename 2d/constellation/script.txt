One star just skipped a beat. Which part of you went quiet?
Across the sky, the stars arrange themselves into a human shape. Each cluster keeps a different rhythm: a heart, lungs, a pulse you cannot hear.
Then the city answers. Streetlights flicker in branching lines, carrying that broken rhythm through roads and windows. The whole night seems to breathe.
Someone looks up. The pattern moves when they move. Their route is written in light across the sky, easy to follow from above.
But one star keeps blinking alone. The Sun. Its pulse matches something beneath the surface. Something that just noticed you looking.
