What if Earth became a moon of Jupiter?
First, the sky changes. Jupiter would fill forty degrees of it, eighty times wider than our Moon.
Next, the Sun fades. Out there, sunlight is twenty-seven times weaker, and the oceans freeze solid.
Jupiter drags you around it in just a day and a half. And one trip around the Sun now takes twelve years.
Every orbit, Jupiter swallows the Sun in an eclipse, and day turns to night.
Then comes tidal force. Jupiter squeezes and stretches the whole planet, again and again.
All that squeezing makes heat. The crust cracks, and volcanoes erupt across the surface, just like on Io.
The planet locks in place. One face always stares at Jupiter, and the other never sees it.
Around Jupiter, belts of radiation would kill an unprotected person in a single day.
Life would struggle. Maybe only deep underground, where it is hidden and warm.
Like Europa today, a frozen world with a secret ocean beneath the ice.
So tell me. Would you move in?
