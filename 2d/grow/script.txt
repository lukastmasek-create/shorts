What if you grew twice as big every two seconds?
Right now, you are one point seven meters tall.
Two seconds. Three point four meters. Your head hits the ceiling.
Ten seconds. Fifty-four meters. Taller than the Statue of Liberty.
But every doubling makes you eight times heavier, and your bones only four times stronger.
Say you survive. At twenty-five seconds, you are taller than Everest.
At thirty-six seconds, you are four hundred kilometers tall. Your own gravity crushes you into a ball.
Forty-six seconds. You are wider than the whole Earth.
Fifty-six seconds. Your head passes the Moon.
One minute. Thirty doublings. You are bigger than the Sun.
Your mass is about forty-five Jupiters. Heavy enough to be a failed star.
Doubling feels small. Thirty steps made you larger than a star.
