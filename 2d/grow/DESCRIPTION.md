What if you doubled in size every two seconds? Start at 1.7 m: 54 m after 10 seconds, taller than Everest at 25, wider than Earth at 46, past the Moon at 56, and bigger than the Sun after one minute (30 doublings, about 1.8 million km). Long before that, the square-cube law would break your bones (weight grows 8x per doubling, bone strength 4x), and anything wider than roughly 400 km collapses into a ball under its own gravity.

Numbers: height 1.7 m x 2^n; Earth diameter 12,742 km; Moon distance 384,400 km; Sun diameter 1.39 million km; Everest 8.85 km; Statue of Liberty 46 m (without pedestal). Mass estimate: 70 kg x 8^30, about 45 Jupiters.
