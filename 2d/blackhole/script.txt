What if you fell into a black hole?
From far away, it is just a dark circle. It pulls no harder than a star of the same mass.
But you drift closer. And you start to fall. Faster. And faster.
Now gravity changes across your own body. Your feet are pulled harder than your head.
That is tidal force. Near a small black hole, you stretch like spaghetti.
Near a giant one, you would not feel a thing.
Meanwhile, time slows down. A friend watching from far away sees you grow slower, dimmer, and redder.
They never see you cross the line.
That line is the event horizon. The point of no return. Even light cannot climb back out.
You cross it. And nothing special happens. No wall. No flash.
But now every path leads inward. The center is no longer a place. It is your future.
Moments later, you reach the singularity. And that is where our physics stops.
So tell me. Would you jump?
