What if you fell into a neutron star?
It is the dead core of a giant star, crushed into a ball just twenty kilometers wide.
Yet it weighs more than our Sun. One spoonful would weigh a billion tons.
You fall toward it. Gravity there is a hundred billion times stronger than on Earth.
You speed up. Faster and faster, until you move at a third of the speed of light.
Before you arrive, tidal force stretches you thin. Your body is pulled apart, atom by atom.
Then you hit the surface. It is smoother than any mirror. The tallest mountain is less than a millimeter high.
The impact is far more violent than any explosion we can build.
In a flash, you are flattened into a single layer of atoms.
Underneath, matter is so dense that protons and electrons merge into neutrons.
And the star keeps spinning, hundreds of times every second.
So tell me. Would you get close?
