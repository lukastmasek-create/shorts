# RobotDreamShort (#92 "What If Robots Started Dreaming?") — fact check
Checked by web search 2026-11-16. Several hits are abstracts/secondary (marked). "Dreaming" is a metaphor in all AI cases; no source claims machine experience.

| Claim | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1994: recordings of hippocampal place cells in 3 rats during maze tasks and slow-wave sleep; waking cell correlations re-appeared in sleep | Wilson & McNaughton, "Reactivation of hippocampal ensemble memories during sleep", Science 265(5172):676-679, 29 Jul 1994, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8036517/ | 3 rats; "re-expressed", read as memory-consolidation trace |
| 1995: wake-sleep algorithm: wake phase adjusts generative weights; sleep phase trains recognition weights on samples the model generates itself | Hinton, Dayan, Frey & Neal, Science 268:1158-1161, 1995, https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/absps/ws.htm | "sleep" is the authors' term |
| June 2015: Google "Inceptionism" / DeepDream: network amplifies patterns it detects in an image or noise; code released 1 Jul 2015 | Mordvintsev, Olah, Tyka, Google Research Blog, https://research.google/blog/inceptionism-going-deeper-into-neural-networks/ | blog date 17 or 18 June in secondary sources; script says "2015" only |
| 2018: agent trained entirely inside its own world-model "dream", policy transferred back to the real environment (CarRacing, VizDoom Take Cover) | Ha & Schmidhuber, World Models, arXiv 1803.10122, NeurIPS 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122 | environments are video games / simulators; abstracts only read |
| Dec 2022: sleep-like (offline, noisy-input, Hebbian) replay recovers old tasks in networks that forgot them | Tadros, Krishnan, Ramyaa, Bazhenov, Nature Communications 13:7742, doi 10.1038/s41467-022-34938-7 | "can recover/reduce", not "solves" forgetting |
| Apr 2025: DreamerV3 learns a world model and improves by imagining futures; first to collect diamonds in Minecraft without human data or curricula | Hafner, Pasukonis, Ba, Lillicrap, Nature, 17 Apr 2025, doi 10.1038/s41586-025-08744-2 | "first" is the authors' claim; press write-ups read, paper itself not fully read; reward shaped by 12 milestones |

## Do not claim
- that any AI dreams in the human sense, has experience, or feels anything (no source supports it)
- DeepDream "is" a dream (blog name; it is image feature amplification)
- exact DeepDream publication day (17/18 June differs)
- that dream-trained agents drove real cars (environments are simulated)
- DreamerV3 needed no rewards (it used milestone rewards)
- "robots" literally dreaming: sources are about neural networks / agents, not physical robots
