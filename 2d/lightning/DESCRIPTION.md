# Description — LightningShort (#85)
**TikTok / Shorts caption:**
What if lightning hit you every single day? 9 in 10 people struck survive. The damage is mostly to the nervous system, and nobody knows what a daily strike would do. Closest case: a park ranger recorded struck 7 times.
