## Do not claim
- Any survival/disability percentage beyond NWS "90% survive" (the "74% permanent disability" figure is unsourced).
- That the body adapts or builds resistance to lightning: no source found; no one has been struck daily. Frame the ending as "nothing in the sources says you could adapt".
- 30,000 K or 30,000 °C: sources conflict/mix units; use NWS 50,000 °F only.
- Sullivan's death, cause of death: do not mention.
- Worldwide death/injury figures (only secondary sources found).
- Exact number of seconds a bolt lasts as a whole (sources give stage timings only).
