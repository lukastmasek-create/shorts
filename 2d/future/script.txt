What if you woke up one billion years in the future?
You open your eyes, and the sky looks wrong. The Sun is brighter, about ten percent hotter than the one you remember.
The air is thin and stale. Oxygen has faded, and the old breath of life is nearly gone.
The continents have crashed together into one giant supercontinent. Every coastline you knew has vanished.
Your cities are gone. Roads, towers, and even mountains have been ground down and buried.
Look up. The Moon has drifted tens of thousands of kilometers away, and looks smaller than before.
It can no longer cover the Sun. Total eclipses are gone forever.
Earth is spinning slower, too. Every day now lasts hours longer.
Every animal you knew is gone. New creatures, shaped by a new world, have taken their place.
The oceans are shrinking, slowly boiling away into the sky.
Humanity left nothing behind. Not a bone, not a book, not a single footprint.
So tell me. Would you go back to sleep?
