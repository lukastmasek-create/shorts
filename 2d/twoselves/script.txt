What if you had two personalities that could see each other?
Real minds are messier. Inner speech can work like a conversation with yourself.
Your future self can feel like a stranger. In a 2009 brain imaging study, people who felt less connected to their future self tended to prefer smaller rewards now.
One brain can even run as two. After surgery cut the link between the hemispheres, split-brain patients acted with each side on its own. The talking side invented reasons for actions it never chose.
Dissociative identity disorder is different. It means two or more distinct identity states, with gaps in memory. It is a recognized disorder, not a superpower.
So no, you do not have two selves watching each other. But you already host more than one voice.
