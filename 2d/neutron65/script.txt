What if you fell into a neutron star? Gravity would tear you apart before you ever touched it.
A neutron star is a dead sun's core, crushed to twenty kilometers wide. One sugar cube of it weighs a billion tons.
You do not drift down. You fall, until you reach two thirds of the speed of light.
Gravity is two hundred billion times stronger than Earth's. Your feet are pulled millions of g's harder than your head, so you stretch like spaghetti.
The magnetic field is a trillion times stronger than any magnet. It squeezes your atoms into needles, and chemistry stops working.
The crust is solid and smoother than a mirror. You land, and you are flattened across it.
But wait. What happens in the exact instant your atoms hit that crust?
That motion has nowhere to go. It becomes hundreds of megatons of heat. You do not die in the dark. You become a flash of X-rays.
