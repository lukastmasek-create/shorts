## YouTube Shorts
Title: What If You Landed on a Diamond Planet? #Shorts

Description:
A planet made entirely of diamond sounds dazzling. But models say the surface would be graphite, the diamond would sit far below, and any oxygen would make a hot diamond world burn. The famous candidate, 55 Cancri e, was once modelled as maybe a third diamond, until its star turned out to be oxygen-rich and JWST found a likely atmosphere. The closest real things: a carbon companion of pulsar PSR J1719-1438, likely crystalline, and the white dwarf "Lucy" with a crystallized carbon core. No planet is confirmed to be made of diamond; this is a what-if.

Sources: Kuchner & Seager, arXiv (2005); Madhusudhan, Lee & Mousis, ApJL (2012); Teske et al., ApJ (2013); Hu et al., Nature (2024); Bailes et al., Science (2011); Metcalfe, Montgomery & Kanaan, ApJL (2004); Shirey & Shigley, Gems & Gemology (2013); U. Bristol, "Diamond" (Molecule of the Month).
