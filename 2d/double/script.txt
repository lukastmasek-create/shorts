What if everyone had an AI double? One that knows you, and answers for you.
In twenty twenty-four, researchers interviewed over a thousand people, about two hours each.
Then they built an AI copy of every person from that one conversation.
Both took the same survey. The copies matched their owners about eighty-five percent as well as the owners matched themselves two weeks later.
And you do not even need an interview. In twenty fifteen, a computer read Facebook likes.
With seventy likes, it judged personality better than a friend. With three hundred, better than a spouse.
Humans still won on subtle traits. But your double would only get better.
Now add your voice and face. In twenty twenty-four, an employee in Hong Kong joined a call with the finance chief.
The finance chief, and every colleague on it, were fakes. The company lost about twenty-five million dollars.
