# DoubleShort (#95 "Every Human Had an AI Double") — fact check
Checked by web search 2026-11-16. Several pages are secondary; primary papers linked. Bars/ticks on screen are schematic unless noted.

| Claim | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2024: Stanford/Northwestern/UW/Google DeepMind team built AI "agents" of 1,000+ real people from a ~2-hour voice interview each | Park et al., "Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People", arXiv:2411.10109 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10109) | preprint; later revisions may differ |
| Those agents matched participants' survey answers about 85% as well as the people matched themselves two weeks later | same paper, General Social Survey result (normalized 0.85; raw ~69% agent vs ~81% self-repeat, per secondary summary) | say "85% as well as", never "85% accurate" |
| 2015: computer predicted personality from Facebook Likes better than a colleague (10 Likes), friend or roommate (70), family member (150), spouse (300) | Youyou, Kosinski & Stillwell, PNAS 112(4):1036–1040, 2015 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4313801) | thresholds via Stanford Daily/GSB summaries; r=0.56 (Likes) vs 0.49 (friends) |
| Humans still did better on traits needing subtle cognition / not evident in digital behaviour | same paper (authors' caveat) | use as honest limit |
| 2024: Hong Kong employee of Arup wired ~US$25 million after a video call where the CFO and colleagues were deepfakes | Arup confirmed; CNN Business / Fortune 2024-05-17 (https://dc.fortune.com/europe/2024/05/17/arup-deepfake-fraud-scam-victim-hong-kong-25-million-cfo/) | sums vary 25–25.6 M; say "about 25 million dollars" |
| Voice cloning needs only a short sample | multiple secondary sources | amount disputed (3 s … 3–5 min) |

## Do not claim
- Any exact seconds of audio for a voice clone (disputed).
- "85% accurate" or that the double "is" you; it was graded on survey answers only.
- That Likes-based prediction works today the same way (2015 study, Facebook data).
- That everyone has or will have a double (hypothetical).
- Raw 69%/81% numbers on screen (secondary summary) — keep to "85% as well as".
