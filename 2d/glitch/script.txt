What if you were trapped in a perfect simulation?
Everything feels real. The sun is warm. Coffee tastes bitter. Your friends make you laugh.
Every detail is rendered just for you, the moment you look at it.
Then one morning, you notice something small. A crow freezes in the air.
You blink, and it flies on, as if nothing happened. But you saw it.
You start testing. The same crack on the sidewalk, again and again. The same stranger, saying the same words.
Every glitch is a clue. The world has edges, and you can find them.
At the edge, the sky is only a texture, and the street just stops.
Behind it, a single line of text: this world is running.
Now you know. The question is, do you tell anyone, or keep living in it?
