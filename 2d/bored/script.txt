What if you knew exactly when someone got bored of you?
Right now, you are guessing.
In 2021, researchers studied 932 conversations. Only about two percent ended when both people wanted them to.
About two thirds of people said there was a moment their conversation should have ended earlier.
Meanwhile, we assume the worst. In five studies, people underestimated how much their partner liked them. Researchers call it the liking gap.
We also think our feelings show more than they do. In a 1998 study, half of the liars thought they were caught. Only a quarter were.
So a boredom meter sounds tempting. But the data say you are probably doing better than you fear.
On average, people like you more than you think.
