# Concept — VirusShort
1. **Zoom ladder.** Each scene is one rung smaller: you, red blood cell, bacterium, cell, virus; every object drawn at its true ratio against a 100 nm "you" marker. Pro: the ratios ARE the content; fits sources. Con: needs care to keep ratios honest.
2. **Journey into the body.** Route through bloodstream to a cell. Con: needs organ facts not in sources.
3. **Countdown to zero.** Shrinking number line only. Con: little to look at.

**Pick 1.** The fixed 100 nm dot of "you" stays on screen while walls (RBC 75x, bacterium 20x, cell 100–300x) get drawn next to it as bars of true proportion. Entry scene: spikes dock into ACE2 receptors (schematic). End on the door metaphor.
Dropped: anything about what the body "looks like" inside (no source).
