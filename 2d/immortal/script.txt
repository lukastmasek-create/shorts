What if you were immortal, but everyone else wasn't?
At first, it feels like a gift. You have all the time in the world.
Then your friends grow old. You attend every goodbye. Their children grow old too.
A hundred years pass. The streets you knew are gone. Even your language changes.
A thousand years. Empires rise and fall. Whole civilizations shrink to a line in a book.
Ten thousand years. Ice ages come and go. Coastlines move. Forests turn to desert.
A million years. Continents drift. Mountains rise, then wear down to dust.
Humans themselves may vanish, and something new takes their place.
A billion years. The sun grows hotter. The oceans boil away. Earth turns to bare rock.
And you are still here. Alone. Watching the last light of the sun.
Immortality is not about living forever. It is about everyone else leaving.
So tell me. Would you take it?
