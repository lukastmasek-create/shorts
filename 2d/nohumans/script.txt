What if you could see the world without humans?
Day one. The lights are still on, but nobody is home.
Within two days, the subway tunnels flood. The pumps have stopped.
After a few years, weeds crack the streets, and roofs begin to leak.
After twenty years, trees grow through the buildings, and wild animals walk the avenues.
After a century, the bridges rust and fall. Rivers run free again.
After a few hundred years, most buildings collapse, and forest covers the city.
Only plastic, glass, and a few statues last for thousands of years.
The planet would be fine. The real question is, would it miss us?
