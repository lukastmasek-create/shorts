What if you could watch the world after every human disappeared?
Day one. Nothing looks different. Then the power grid starts to fail. City by city, the lights go out.
Within days, the pumps stop. Subway tunnels flood. Streets turn into rivers.
A few years. Weeds crack the pavement. Roots lift the sidewalks. Grass swallows the roads.
A few decades. Roofs rot. Houses collapse. Rusted bridges drop into the water.
The animals come back. Deer, wolves and birds walk through empty cities.
A few centuries. Forests grow where skyscrapers once stood.
Everything we built slowly turns back into soil. Except the plastic. It lasts for hundreds of years.
At night, with no city lights, the sky fills with stars again.
The planet keeps going without us. And it looks healthier.
So here is the real question. What will we leave behind?
