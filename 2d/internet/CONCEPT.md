# Concept — #98 What If You Could Enter the Internet?
Pitches (MONO, white line art, no faces, ~65 s):
A. **Tourist guide**: map of an imaginary "city of websites", each district crossed out and swapped for its real thing. Fun, but needs invented geography; weak on facts.
B. **Follow one tap**: one glowing dot leaves a phone, rides radio, glass, seabed cable, lands in a data center, returns. One continuous route left-to-right, every beat a real fact. Strong spine, easy camera whip.
C. **Timeline of bricks**: 1858 -> 1969 -> 1991 -> today. Educational but flat; history repeats other shorts.
**Pick B.** Answers the premise ("you can't enter a city, but you can follow the road") and every scene maps to SOURCES.md rows. Dot = hero object; person icon only at the start/end. Dropped: first-website date (sources disagree), 99% claim.
