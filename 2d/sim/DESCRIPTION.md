## YouTube Shorts
Title: What If You Were the Only Real Human? (What Science Says) #Shorts

Description:
What if everyone else were a simulation? Here is what philosophy and psychology actually say, with no made-up claims.

• Solipsism and the problem of other minds: you only experience your own mind and infer everyone else's from behavior.
• Nick Bostrom (2003): at least one of three statements is true: civilizations die out, they never run simulations, or we are probably in one. He does not claim which.
• Spotlight effect (Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky, 2000): people overestimate how much others notice them.
• Truman Show delusion (Gold & Gold, 2012): a described delusion, not an official diagnosis.
• Dunn, Aknin & Norton (Science, 2008): spending money on others raised happiness more than spending on yourself.
