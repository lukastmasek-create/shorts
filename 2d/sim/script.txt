What if you were the only real human?
It starts small. A stranger says the exact sentence you heard yesterday.
A crowd crosses the street. Same steps. Same pauses. Same rhythm.
You look closer. Faces repeat. Nobody ever starts a thought you did not start.
So you test it. You say something no one could predict. Silence. Then an answer. Too fast. Too perfect.
You turn around quickly. Everyone is already looking at you.
Now the world feels like a stage. Built around one person. You.
But here is the problem. You cannot prove they are fake. And you cannot prove they are real.
So choose. If you are wrong, kindness costs you nothing. If you are right, it is the only thing that is still real.
Be kind. Just in case.
