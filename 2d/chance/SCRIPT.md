What if you could control probability?
Every coin lands heads. Every die rolls six.
Every lottery ticket wins. You are rich by lunch.
Even the impossible gets cheap. One chance in a trillion becomes as easy as a coin flip.
In theory, you could walk through a wall. Particles can slip through barriers, with a tiny chance.
But one careless wish, and every air molecule in the room drifts into one corner.
The whole universe runs on chance. Atoms decay by chance. Even the Sun shines because of a lucky quantum leap.
And a life with no chance? No luck. No surprise. No game worth playing.
Control every chance, and you lose the surprise.
