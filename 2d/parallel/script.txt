What if you could enter a parallel universe?
At first, everything looks the same. Same sky. Same streets. Same faces.
Then you notice small things. A coin with the wrong face. A date you do not remember.
You follow the clues back to the day the two worlds split. One tiny decision.
In your world, a young inventor missed her train. In this one, she caught it.
On that train, she met a stranger who believed in her idea.
A small machine, built in a garage. Nobody laughed this time.
Within ten years, every home had one. Cities grew clean and quiet.
Within fifty, people were walking on other worlds.
But there is a price. Nobody looks up anymore. They already live out there.
You step back through, and you are on the platform again. The next train is coming.
One tiny decision. What will you change?
