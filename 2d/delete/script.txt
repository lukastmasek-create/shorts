What if you could delete embarrassing memories?
Science has tried. In a famous experiment, people learned word pairs, then practiced not thinking about some of them. Later, they remembered those words less.
Researchers also tried a drug. In a 2009 study, propranolol, given while a fear memory was reactivated, reduced the fear response a day later. Some later studies failed to repeat it.
But you may not need either. Negative feelings fade faster than positive ones. Psychologists call it the fading affect bias.
Memory is not a recording. Each memory is rebuilt when you recall it, inside a system tied to who you are.
So delete enough, and you edit who you are.
Time already dulls the sting, and keeps the story.
