What if every clock stopped at the same time?
Wall clocks, phones, watches. All frozen at three seventeen.
Nobody reacts. People walk past the frozen clocks as if nothing is wrong.
You look up. The sun still moves. Your heart still beats. Time has not stopped. Only the clocks.
Trains miss their slots. Flights stay on the ground. Banks never open. The world slowly jams.
Yet nobody panics, because they trust the clock more than the sun.
Dinner time comes. You are hungry. Everyone around you insists it is still three seventeen.
You point at the sunset. They only look at the clock.
A clock does not measure time. It only tells us what to agree on.
If everyone agrees, who is right? Do you speak up, or go along?
