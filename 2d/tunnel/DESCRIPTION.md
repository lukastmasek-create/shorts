## YouTube Shorts
Title: What If You Dropped a Ball Through the Earth? #Shorts

Description:
A ball dropped into a frictionless tunnel through the Earth would swing back and forth like a pendulum. In the textbook uniform-density model one trip takes about 42 minutes, and any straight tunnel takes the same time. A 2015 calculation with real seismic data gives about 38 minutes. Earth's spin and air would spoil the ideal picture.

Sources: Klotz, American Journal of Physics 83, 231 (2015); PREM gravity profile; Kola Superdeep Borehole (12,262 m); gravity-train physics (uniform-density model).
