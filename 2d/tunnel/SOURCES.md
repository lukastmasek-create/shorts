## Do NOT claim
- Any exact speed other than "near eight km/s (simple model)"; the real peak differs.
- Any sideways-drift number; any claim the tunnel could be built (heat, pressure, rock flow not sourced).
- Temperatures/pressures of the core (not researched).
- That Kola is the deepest *point* reached by any drill by length (Qatar well is longer).
