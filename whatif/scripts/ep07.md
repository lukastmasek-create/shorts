# Episode 07 — What if your reflection lived one day ahead?

Hook: **WHAT IF YOUR REFLECTION LIVED ONE DAY AHEAD?**  
Length: 94.9 s (2847 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–11.9 s
What if your reflection lived one day ahead? You brush your teeth, glance up, and your reflection is already wearing a party hat and eating cake. That is tomorrow's you.

## Scene 2 · 11.9–23.8 s
Soon it gets useful. Your reflection holds up a sign: bus nine will be late. You leave later, finish your tea, and still arrive exactly on time. Smugness level: maximum.

## Scene 3 · 23.8–35.0 s
Then it gets greedy. Your reflection waves a lottery ticket. You copy the numbers, buy the ticket, and feel like the smartest person in history. Hypothetically, of course.

## Scene 4 · 35.0–46.5 s
But then you change your mind. The reflection freezes, scratches its head, and holds up a sign: that is not what I did. Apparently, tomorrow is only a suggestion.

## Scene 5 · 46.5–58.0 s
You start to notice how tired it looks. It lives every day before you do, and never gets surprised. It holds up one last sign: I miss not knowing.

## Scene 6 · 58.0–69.9 s
One morning the sign says something different: call Grandpa today. You don't ask why. You pick up the phone and talk for an hour. Some forecasts are better as warnings.

## Scene 7 · 69.9–82.6 s
Then comes the twist. The reflection steps out of the mirror and sits down beside you. It was never ahead of you. It was just patiently waiting for you to catch up.

## Scene 8 · 82.6–94.9 s
Maybe that's the real thought experiment. The future isn't a prophecy in the glass. It's someone who loves you, standing a day ahead, saying: come on, I saved you a seat.
