# Episode 10 — What if Earth had a ten-second rewind button?

Hook: **WHAT IF EARTH HAD A 10-SECOND REWIND BUTTON?**  
Length: 93.9 s (2817 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–14.1 s
What if Earth had a ten-second rewind button? You spill coffee on your laptop, panic, and slap the big red button on the lamp post. The whole planet rewinds. The coffee flies back into your cup.

## Scene 2 · 14.1–24.5 s
Everyone walks backward for ten seconds. Pigeons un-poop. A delivery truck un-honks. Nobody remembers it except you, standing there holding the button like a magic remote.

## Scene 3 · 24.5–35.7 s
You start fixing small things. A trip on the stairs. A typo in a text. An awkward hello. With ten seconds, you become the smoothest person in town.

## Scene 4 · 35.7–46.9 s

## Scene 5 · 46.9–58.1 s
Arguments get weird. You say something rude, slap the button, and the words slide back into your mouth. Honestly, this should have been invented for every family dinner.

## Scene 6 · 58.1–70.0 s
Scientists say a real rewind would need to reverse entropy itself, which is, to put it politely, impossible. But as a thought experiment, it is a lovely idea to hold.

## Scene 7 · 70.0–82.7 s
Twist: you are the only one who remembers. So you save the button for one moment: your niece's very first laugh. You rewind it again and again, until the day finally ends.

## Scene 8 · 82.7–93.9 s
Then you let go. Time moves forward, soft and unstoppable. The button sits quietly on the lamp post, waiting for a moment you would never want to lose.
