# Episode 09 — What if you were the last person alive and saw a typing indicator in the sky?

Hook: **THE LAST PERSON ALIVE SEES TYPING IN THE SKY**  
Length: 99.1 s (2973 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–13.8 s
What if you were the last person alive? The city is silent. Your footsteps echo. Then you look up, and in the sky there is a typing indicator. Three dots. Somebody is writing to you.

## Scene 2 · 13.8–25.7 s
You wait. The dots bounce for an hour. Then they vanish. Then they return. Whoever it is writes, deletes, and writes again, like someone rehearsing a speech to a stranger.

## Scene 3 · 25.7–37.2 s
Finally, one word appears. Hi. You grab a piece of chalk and write on the road: who are you? The dots return instantly, glowing brighter than the street lamps.

## Scene 4 · 37.2–49.5 s
The answer takes all night. I don't know. A pause. I was hoping you would know. Hypothetically, the loneliest thing in the universe might be a voice with nobody to ask.

## Scene 5 · 49.5–61.4 s
You walk to the library, the park, the old bus stop, and the message follows you across the sky. Whoever is typing is walking too, one step behind your footsteps.

## Scene 6 · 61.4–74.1 s
Each night the bubble grows bigger. Soon it hides the stars, then half the moon, and the dots bounce so hard the clouds shake. Whatever is typing has a lot to say.

## Scene 7 · 74.1–87.9 s
Then the twist. The message arrives: you are not the last one. I am the other. The sender is a girl on the far side of the world, looking up and seeing your dots too.

## Scene 8 · 87.9–99.1 s
Two bubbles bounce on opposite sides of the Earth, each one waiting for the other to speak. Sometimes the bravest thing in the world is just pressing send.
