# Episode 02 — What if gravity flipped whenever your phone buzzed?

Hook: **WHAT IF GRAVITY FLIPPED WHEN YOUR PHONE BUZZED?**  
Length: 89.0 s (2670 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–10.4 s
What if gravity flipped every time your phone buzzed? You are happily eating a sandwich in the park. Bzzt. And suddenly, the sky is the floor.

## Scene 2 · 10.4–21.2 s
Gravity flips again at the café. Cups rocket to the ceiling, chairs follow, and the barista calmly asks whether anyone wants to keep their oat milk latte.

## Scene 3 · 21.2–32.0 s
Then the group chat wakes up. Twenty-three notifications, each one a flip. Up, down, up again. You become a very confused pinball with a very full inbox.

## Scene 4 · 32.0–43.9 s
On the highway it gets worse. Cars float up like startled fish. Drivers wave, hold their steering wheels, and admit that this is a bad moment to receive a text.

## Scene 5 · 43.9–55.1 s
You switch on airplane mode. Great idea, except everyone else didn't. So you hug a lamp post while the whole town flips, and ten thousand phones sing together.

## Scene 6 · 55.1–65.9 s
Physicists would call this pure fiction, since a phone cannot change gravity. But as a thought experiment, a flipping vector would make every day a roller coaster.

## Scene 7 · 65.9–77.8 s
Then the twist. Your battery hits one percent, and the phone dies. Nobody can text you. Gravity stays flipped forever, and the whole town is now living on the sky.

## Scene 8 · 77.8–89.0 s
So everyone learns to walk on clouds, plug in their chargers, and keep their phones nearby. Now nobody dares to send you a message. Not even your mum.
