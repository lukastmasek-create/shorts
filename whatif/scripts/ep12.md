# Episode 12 — What if your phone showed tomorrow's photos?

Hook: **WHAT IF YOUR PHONE SHOWED TOMORROW'S PHOTOS?**  
Length: 100.5 s (3015 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–11.5 s
What if your phone showed tomorrow's photos? You open the gallery and there's a picture you haven't taken yet: you, covered in cake, standing in a very confused crowd.

## Scene 2 · 11.5–23.4 s
So you dodge the cake. Next morning, you spot the exact same room, but now it's perfectly calm. The photo changed. Apparently, tomorrow only exists until you look at it.

## Scene 3 · 23.4–37.2 s
Photos become a habit. A winning goal. A new puppy. A rainy day with no umbrella. You carry an umbrella. You win a goal. Your friends call you psychic, and you politely refuse a hat.

## Scene 4 · 37.2–50.2 s
Then one photo makes your stomach drop. It's your grandfather's garden, empty, with one chair left in the grass. You stare at it for a long while before you realise: you have time.

## Scene 5 · 50.2–64.0 s
You head to the garden. He is there, humming over the tomatoes, and you stay for hours. The new photo shows two chairs, two cups, and a very smug tomato. Some futures can be edited.

## Scene 6 · 64.0–76.3 s
Scientists would say a phone cannot see the future, because the future hasn't happened yet. In this thought experiment, the gallery is less a crystal ball and more a gentle suggestion.

## Scene 7 · 76.3–88.6 s
Here is the twist. One last photo appears: you, today, standing right here, holding your phone, staring at that exact photo. You look up, and the camera in the corner blinks.

## Scene 8 · 88.6–100.5 s
So you pocket the phone, grin at the camera, and strike a ridiculous pose. Tomorrow's photo updates at once: you laughing, sunrise behind you, with absolutely no cake in sight.
