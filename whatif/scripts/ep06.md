Hook: **WHAT IF YOUR DREAMS LEAKED INTO MORNING?**

## Scene 1 · 0.0–11.9 s
What if your dreams leaked into the morning? You fall asleep dreaming of a flying whale. A bubble drifts from your head, slips through the window, and starts to grow.

## Scene 2 · 11.9–22.0 s
Morning. You wake up, and the whale is still there. It drifts across your bedroom, humming. Your cat, unimpressed, files this under normal Tuesday activity.

## Scene 3 · 22.0–32.1 s

## Scene 4 · 32.1–42.9 s

## Scene 5 · 42.9–54.1 s
Then the nightmares start to leak too. A scribbly monster squeezes out of the neighbor's dream, towers over the street, and growls, and everyone runs for the bushes.

## Scene 6 · 54.1–66.0 s
You stand your ground, take a breath, and offer the monster a hug. It blinks, shrinks, and purrs. Nightmares, it turns out, only stay huge while you run from them.

## Scene 7 · 66.0–77.5 s
And here's the twist. The whale isn't your dream at all. Gently floating above the rooftops, it's fast asleep, and in its own enormous bubble it's dreaming about you.

## Scene 8 · 77.5–89.0 s
So you climb a ladder of morning light, hop onto the whale's back, and sail across the sunrise together. The dream is leaking, sure. But so is the wonder.
