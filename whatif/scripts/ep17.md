# Episode 17 — What if objects remembered everyone who touched them?

Hook: **WHAT IF OBJECTS REMEMBERED WHO TOUCHED THEM?**  
Length: 102.5 s (3075 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–13.8 s
What if objects remembered everyone who touched them? In a dusty antique shop, you pick up an old blue mug, and a crowd of glowing ghostly figures appears behind you, each holding the same mug.

## Scene 2 · 13.8–26.5 s
Doorknobs are worse. A thousand hands squeeze the same knob in a glowing queue that stretches around the corner. You only want to go inside, and the ghosts politely wait their turn.

## Scene 3 · 26.5–38.8 s
A coin flashes through town. You can see its whole journey in glowing footsteps: a busker, a baker, a child, a magician, and now, you. Nobody ever kept it for long.

## Scene 4 · 38.8–51.5 s
In the elevator, the button glows with everyone who ever pushed it. Impatient ghosts jab it ten times. Polite ghosts tap it gently. One suspicious ghost just keeps staring at the ceiling.

## Scene 5 · 51.5–65.6 s
Back home, your old teddy bear remembers everything. A tiny glowing you hugs it on the first day of school. Another glowing you hugs it after a bad dream, and another after your very first goodbye.

## Scene 6 · 65.6–76.4 s
Scientists say atoms can't remember anything, so this is just a thought experiment. Still, imagine every object quietly holding the warmth of the hands that touched it.

## Scene 7 · 76.4–88.7 s
Here's the twist. In Grandma's sewing box you find a silver thimble, and it remembers her best. A soft glowing Grandma appears, stitching a scarf, humming the song she always hummed.

## Scene 8 · 88.7–102.5 s
She puts her glowing hand over yours, and for one gentle moment, you can almost feel it. Maybe nothing truly leaves us. Maybe the world just keeps every touch, softly, in the things we love.
