# Episode 01 — What if your shadow walked away?

Hook: **WHAT IF YOUR SHADOW WALKED AWAY?**  
Length: 108.8 s (3264 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–13.7 s
What if your shadow simply walked away? No warning, no goodbye. One second it's glued to your feet. The next, it's strolling down the street like it has somewhere to be.

## Scene 2 · 13.7–27.4 s
You chase it. It dodges a dog, tips an invisible hat, and buys a coffee. Somehow a shadow holding a latte feels so much worse than a shadow with no plan.

## Scene 3 · 27.4–40.6 s
Then it catches the number nine bus. You catch your breath. Scientists say a shadow is only missing light, so hypothetically it cannot ride a bus. Nobody told the shadow.

## Scene 4 · 40.6–53.8 s
Then word spreads. Across town, other shadows slip off their owners and join the march. Soon it's a parade, and thousands of people stand in plain sunlight, feeling strangely exposed.

## Scene 5 · 53.8–68.3 s
By sunset you give up. Without a shadow, bright afternoons feel weirdly empty, and every lamp post looks smug. You only want to go home, lock the door, and forget this ever happened.

## Scene 6 · 68.3–80.7 s
You trudge home through the dark. And then you see it. Your living room light is already on. Someone is inside. A curtain twitches, and the silhouette waves.

## Scene 7 · 80.7–93.9 s
Twist: the shadow got home first. It's wearing your slippers, holding your keys, and it already made tea. It looks at the clock, then at you, and says: you're late.

## Scene 8 · 93.9–108.8 s
So whose shadow was it all along? A thought experiment, of course. But tonight, glance at the floor. Maybe it was never following you. Maybe it was just waiting for you to come home.
