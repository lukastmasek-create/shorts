# Episode 08 — What if everyone could see your thoughts?

Hook: **WHAT IF EVERYONE COULD SEE YOUR THOUGHTS?**  
Length: 91.0 s (2730 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–11.9 s
What if everyone could see your thoughts? You step outside, and every head wears a bubble. Pizza. Late again. Cute dog. And yours, in huge letters: why is everyone staring?

## Scene 2 · 11.9–24.6 s
On a first date, things get awkward fast. He thinks she is wonderful. She thinks, don't spill the tea. Both blush at once, which is the most honest first date in history.

## Scene 3 · 24.6–35.4 s
At work, Monday morning becomes a thriller. The boss thinks, I hate meetings. Karen thinks, free lunch? And Dave's bubble reads: updating my CV. Somebody coughs politely.

## Scene 4 · 35.4–45.1 s
Exams are tricky too. A student wonders about answer B, and the teacher reads it instantly. Cheating is impossible, but strangely everyone's handwriting improves.

## Scene 5 · 45.1–55.2 s
Poker night is chaos. Nobody can bluff when their thoughts read, I have two sevens. The game collapses into a very friendly conversation about snacks.

## Scene 6 · 55.2–67.9 s
Try not to think about a pink elephant, and suddenly the entire town is wearing pink elephants above their heads. Trying not to think about something is the loudest thought of all.

## Scene 7 · 67.9–79.1 s
Here's the twist. Once everyone sees each other's thoughts, they discover the same secret. Everyone is nervous, hopeful, and secretly hoping you like them. Almost nobody is judging.

## Scene 8 · 79.1–91.0 s
So the whole town gathers in the square for one enormous hug. A thousand bubbles float above them, and every single one shows the same shape: a small, shy heart.
