# Episode 05 — What if every blink skipped one second?

Hook: **WHAT IF EVERY BLINK SKIPPED ONE SECOND?**  
Length: 90.8 s (2724 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–12.3 s
What if every blink skipped one second? You step off the curb, blink, and the bus that was far away is suddenly right in your face. Blink again, and it's gone.

## Scene 2 · 12.3–23.8 s
Sports get strange. You throw a basketball, blink, and miss the whole arc. You never see the ball fly. You only ever see the ball arrive, or fail to.

## Scene 3 · 23.8–33.1 s
Cooking becomes risky. Blink, the toast is golden. Blink, it's charcoal. Blink again, and the smoke alarm is singing a very high note.

## Scene 4 · 33.1–45.0 s
So you try never to blink. Staring contests become your new sport. But your eyes dry out like raisins, and your best friend tells jokes just to make you crack.

## Scene 5 · 45.0–55.4 s
Do the math, hypothetically. About fifteen blinks a minute means fifteen seconds skipped. That's a quarter of your waking life, gone before you even finish breakfast.

## Scene 6 · 55.4–66.6 s
At your birthday, you blink while blowing out the candles. The party jumps ahead. The cake is gone, the balloons are drooping, and everyone has politely gone home.

## Scene 7 · 66.6–78.9 s
But here's the twist. A night of sleep is just one long blink. You'd wake up exactly one second later, rested, and the world would not have moved on at all.

## Scene 8 · 78.9–90.8 s
So you sit on a bench, close your eyes, and let the whole city stand still for a heartbeat. When you open them, the sunset has painted everything gold. Blink.
