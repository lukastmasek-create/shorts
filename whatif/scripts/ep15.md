# Episode 15 — What if all shadows vanished at noon, except one?

Hook: **WHAT IF ALL SHADOWS VANISHED AT NOON... EXCEPT ONE?**  
Length: 96.1 s (2883 frames @ 30 fps), 8 scenes

## Scene 1 · 0.0–11.9 s
What if all shadows vanished at noon, except one? The sun reaches the top of the sky. Every shadow on the street shrinks, flickers, and pops, like a soap bubble.

## Scene 2 · 11.9–24.2 s
People gasp, then shrug. Hot sidewalks get hotter, and picnic blankets need hats. But in the middle of the plaza, one single shadow stays put, sharp as ink, and it's yours.

## Scene 3 · 24.2–36.9 s
Within minutes, the whole town is crowding around you. Everyone is hot, and your shadow is the only shade on Earth. Soon forty neighbors are squished beneath a shadow shaped like you.

## Scene 4 · 36.9–47.7 s
Scientists would say shadows can't just disappear, because they're only the absence of light. In this thought experiment, the missing shade feels like missing a quiet friend.

## Scene 5 · 47.7–59.2 s
Mayors panic. Newspapers write headlines like, one shadow to rule them all. You just want to buy ice cream, but your shadow keeps tugging you gently down the street.

## Scene 6 · 59.2–70.4 s
The shadow finally stops at an empty lot where nothing grows. It stretches, wriggles, and settles into a perfect little circle. Then it waits, patient as a seed.

## Scene 7 · 70.4–83.8 s
Here is the twist. A tiny green sprout pushes up through the middle of the shadow. It was never your shadow. It was the shadow of the tree this seed would become, one day.

## Scene 8 · 83.8–96.1 s
So the whole town waters the sprout. Years later it's a huge, leafy tree with a shadow wide enough for everyone, and every noon, the shadows return to sit beneath it.
