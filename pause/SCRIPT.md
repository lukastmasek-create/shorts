# What If You Could Pause Everyone Except Yourself For 10 Minutes A Day?

## Facts (real, math checked)
- 10 min x 365 days = 3,650 min = about 60.8 hours per year (2.5 days).
- 10 min x 365 x 80 years = 292,000 min = about 203 days.
- 203 days = about 0.55 year: you end up ~half a year biologically older than everyone your age (you live the paused minutes, they do not).
- 10 min is 1/144 of a day (0.69%).
- Average US one-way commute is about 27 minutes (US Census, ACS).
- Thought experiment, not science: no real mechanism exists to pause time for others.

## Voice-over (English, one steady tone, ~210 words)

HOOK
Every day, the world freezes for ten minutes. Everyone except you.

SETTING
No traffic. No noise. A pigeon hangs in the air like a sticker.

ACTION
On day one, you skip the line at the coffee shop. Nobody notices.
By day ten, you finish your homework. Then your neighbour's.
By day one hundred, you have read a whole library, while the pigeon watches.
By year ten, you know every frozen stranger in town.

SNAP
Then one morning, you look in the mirror. Your hair is greyer than your friends'.

DEADPAN
Luckily, you gain sixty extra hours every year.
Unluckily, you are the only one who ages during them.

EXPLANATION
Here is the math. Ten minutes a day is sixty hours a year.
Over eighty years, that is about two hundred days.
Everyone else lives eighty years. You live eighty years and half a year more.

CONSEQUENCE
You are always a little older than your friends. A little wiser. A little more tired.
And nobody can ever explain why.

CALLBACK TWIST
So at the end, they ask for your secret. You smile and say: ten minutes.
Then you do what you always do with them.
You close your eyes. And the whole world waits for you.
